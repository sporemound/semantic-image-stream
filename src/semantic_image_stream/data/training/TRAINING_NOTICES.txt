IMAGE TRAINING SOURCE: Mitsua/art-museums-pd-440k

Revision fba945da78b36262eb9272067197cc28d06cffbf, original train split.
320 deduplicated images: 240 fitting, 40 calibration, 40 untouched test.
Original museum images/metadata are identified as CC0; compiled English
captions and dataset are CC BY 4.0. The accompanying training-manifest.json
retains each title, creator, source URL, caption, license, row index and hashes.
The sampled pixels are not redistributed in this package. The model contains
learned scalar calibration parameters; original CLIP weights are not included.
Changes: bounded sampling, raster conversion, pHash deduplication, source-group
splits, caption-pair positives and weak wrong-source negatives, local fitting.
This is a caption-pair benchmark, not evidence of better audiobook matching.

STORY EXAMPLES: FairytaleQAData / UCI School of Education contributors.
https://github.com/uci-soe/FairytaleQAData
Endorsed Hugging Face copy: WorkInTheDark/FairytaleQA
https://huggingface.co/datasets/WorkInTheDark/FairytaleQA
Revision 655b20b370f8ec6ef0cae5ac08f1c27ec6e72aaf, train.csv only.
128 explicit, literally supported human question-answer examples, selected
from character/setting/action/outcome annotations. Apache License 2.0 for
the upstream dataset; full license accompanies this file.
Original fairy-tale text comes from the public-domain Gutenberg collection;
story rights and applicable source conditions remain separate. Source story
names and annotation row numbers are retained in every example.
https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html
Changes: train-only subset, maximum two examples per source story, literal
section/answer validation, bounded local retrieval and prompt excerpts.
These illustrate reasoning and cannot establish facts about a user's book.
No Qwen fine-tuning was performed. No private narration was used or uploaded.
